# The Ultimate Tracker

One app for everything you read, watch, cook, clean and feel: **books, movies, TV shows, recipes, home chores, and cycle & pregnancy tracking**.

**Open it:** https://annabxclu.github.io/The-Ultimate-Tracker/

On your phone, add it to your home screen so it opens like a regular app:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ → *Install app*

## Features

### 📚 Books
- Search millions of books (title, author or ISBN) via Open Library, or add one by hand
- Shelves: **Currently Reading**, **To Be Read** and **Finished**, each with a fanned cover preview, the book count and an arrow to open the full list
- Page-by-page progress with quick +10 / +25 buttons
- Yearly **reading goal** with a progress ring and an ahead/behind-schedule indicator
- **Milestones**: first book, 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 books, and 1k–50k pages read
- Ratings, notes, start/finish dates, and your own cover photo when one is missing

### 🎬 Movies
- Search movies (Apple's iTunes catalog) or add by hand
- Lists: **In Progress** (with "where you left off"), **Movie List** and **Finished**
- Ratings, notes, add or replace the poster with your own photo

### 📺 TV shows
- Search shows (TVMaze) and the full episode list is loaded automatically
- Lists: **In Progress**, **Show List** and **Finished**
- Check off **episode by episode** or a **whole season** at once
- "Up next" card to tick off the next episode in one tap
- Shows move to In Progress / Finished automatically as you check episodes
- Refresh to pull in newly announced episodes; manual shows can have seasons and episodes added by hand

### 🍝 Recipes
- **Paste a recipe link** and the app keeps only the title, photo, ingredients and steps (no life story)
- If a recipe has no photo, add your own
- Tap the cart next to any ingredient, or **Add all to cart**
- Shopping cart grouped by recipe, check items off, add extras, share the list, and jump to **Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh, Target or Kroger** to buy each item
- Tap a step to mark it done while you cook
- On Android, share a recipe page straight into the installed app

### 🧹 Chores
- Add the **rooms** in your home (one-tap presets like Kitchen, Bathroom, Bedroom, Yard, or make your own with an icon)
- Add **chores to each room**, with suggestions tailored to the room
- Set how often: **daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or annually**
- Each chore shows the **date it was last done** and the **date it's next due**; next due is worked out automatically and you can change it
- **Checkbox** to mark a chore done: it logs today and schedules the next date (tap again to undo)
- Home screen shows what's **overdue**, **due today** and **coming up this week** across every room

### 🌸 Bloom: cycle & pregnancy
**Cycle mode**
- Cycle ring showing **which day and phase you're in** (period, follicular, ovulation window, luteal) and what's happening in your body, with tips for that phase
- **Predictions**: next period, fertile window and ovulation date, which get better the more you log
- Week strip and monthly **calendar** with logged and predicted periods, fertile days and ovulation
- Daily log of flow, symptoms, mood and notes
- **Cycle history**: every cycle's length and period length, plus your average cycle, average period and how regular you are

*Bloom is off by default. Turn it on in **Settings → Tabs**.*

**Pregnancy mode** (toggle at the top of the tab; your cycle data is kept)
- Different look: soft lavender instead of rose
- How far along you are (e.g. "12 weeks, 3 days"), your trimester, a countdown and due date
- **Baby size each week** (e.g. a lime at week 12) with approximate length and weight
- What's happening with the baby, your body, and a tip for every week from 4 to 40; tap the arrows to browse any week
- **Bump diary**: add a photo each week and watch your bump grow

*Predictions and pregnancy info are general estimates. They aren't birth control or medical advice.*

## Make it yours
**Settings → Tabs**: switch any tab on or off, and drag the ≡ handles to reorder them. You can also **press and hold a tab in the bottom bar and drag it** to a new spot.

**Settings → Background color**: choose from 15 preset shades (light pastels through dark slate, forest, plum and night) or pick any custom color. Text, cards and accents adjust automatically so dark backgrounds stay readable, and your choice is remembered.

## Your data
Everything is stored **privately on your device** (no account, no server). Use **Settings → Export** to back up or move to a new phone, and **Import backup** to restore.

## Notes
- Recipe import works on most recipe sites (anything that publishes standard recipe data). It reads the page through public relay services because browsers block apps from reading other sites directly. If a site refuses, tap **Add it manually** and the form is prefilled with what was found.
- Built as a single HTML file with no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
